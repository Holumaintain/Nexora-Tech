Frequently asked questions

Migration questions, answered.

What can we migrate to Nexora?

Migration can cover the projects, issues, team structure, workflows, and product context that are important to your organization. The exact migration approach depends on the systems and information you are moving from.

Do we have to stop using our existing tools immediately?

No. A transition can be structured around your organization's workflow. Existing services can remain part of the process while teams progressively move their core work into Nexora.

Can Nexora connect to our existing tools?

Nexora is designed around connected workflows. Explore the integrations available across communication, development, productivity, analytics, cloud, and automation workflows.

Can your team help us plan the migration?

Yes. Contact the Nexora team to discuss your current environment, migration requirements, and the workflow you want to build after the transition.
