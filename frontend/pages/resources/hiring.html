Hiring process

A thoughtful process, not a guessing game.

We want candidates to understand what to expect and have enough context to show us how they think and work.

01

Intro conversation

Start with a conversation about your background, interests, experience, and what you are looking for next.

02

Role deep dive

Meet people from the team and explore the problems, responsibilities, and expectations associated with the role.

03

Practical session

Depending on the role, you may work through a practical exercise or discuss a realistic problem relevant to the position.

04

Team conversation

Meet additional teammates and learn more about how we collaborate, communicate, and make decisions.

05

Decision

We bring everything together, share next steps, and make a clear decision without unnecessary delays.

Candidates

Questions about joining us?

What should I include in my application?

Include your CV or resume, relevant work samples where appropriate, and a short explanation of why the role interests you. Keep it clear and focused on the work you would like to do.

Can I apply if there isn't an exact role for me?

Yes. If you believe your experience could contribute to Nexora, use the general application option and tell us what you would like to build or improve.

What happens after I apply?

Applications are reviewed based on the requirements of the role. Candidates whose experience aligns with the opportunity may be invited to an introductory conversation.

Do you hire remotely?

Working arrangements can vary by role and team. The specific location and working expectations will be communicated as part of the hiring process.
